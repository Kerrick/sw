# Homeworld: Luke is from Tatooine, and Yoda's homeworld is unknown

Estimate: 20 minutes

The homeworld box in Selected Character, from the planet record. It is a
View-only part of the Selected Character panel.

At 120×40, Selected Character has 10 rows inside, because the Starships and
Vehicles row is tall enough for Obi-Wan's five starships. Homeworld, Species,
and Attributes share those rows.

- [x] Luke's homeworld resolves to Tatooine, with population, terrain, climate, surface water, diameter, rotation and orbital periods, and gravity
- [x] A planet named `"unknown"` (planets/28, Yoda's) shows as an unknown homeworld, not a place
- [x] Planet numbers show with separators, as the wireframe does: `"200000"` as 200,000 and `"10465"` as 10,465 km; `"unknown"` as unknown
- [x] A diameter, rotation period, or orbital period of `"0"` shows as unknown (Polis Massa's diameter; planets/28's periods)
- [x] A planet's residents link back, and both directions agree
- [x] Dagobah's gravity `"N/A"` reads dimmed, like unknown
- [x] Tund's population of `"0"` shows as 0, since the Sorcerers of Tund's last member killed everyone on it
