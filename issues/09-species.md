# Species: Luke's species is unknown

Estimate: 20 minutes

The species box in Selected Character, a View-only part of that panel. Thirty-two characters, Luke among
them, have no species in the data. The app shows them as unknown and never
guesses: R4-P17 is a droid and Sly Moore an Umbaran.

Species follows Homeworld, in the middle column. Selected Character has no
row to spare, so the label and the name share the border title, "Species:
**Human**". Units follow the wireframe: "180 cm" and "120 yr". An unknown
species shows dimmed through `Sw::Empty`, as Yoda's unknown planet does.

- [x] Luke's empty species shows as unknown
- [ ] Species/1 (Human) doesn't list Luke either, so the two directions agree on his missing species, pinned by a test
- [ ] A droid's average lifespan of "indefinite" reads with no "yr"
