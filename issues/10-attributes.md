# Attributes: Jabba weighs 1,358 kg, and R2-D2's hair doesn't apply

Estimate: 25 minutes

The attributes table in Selected Character, where most of the data's quirks
live. It is a View-only part of that panel, named `AttributesBox` like the
other boxes in it.

Attributes takes the left column, beside Species and Homeworld, with the
wireframe's units: "172 cm" and "77 kg". Every panel already dims `"n/a"`,
as it does `"unknown"`, so R2-D2's items are about telling `"n/a"` from
`"none"`, not about dimming.

- [x] Luke's attributes: 19BBY, male, 172 cm, 77 kg, blue eyes, blond hair, and fair skin
- [ ] Birth years `"19BBY"`, `"41.9BBY"`, and `"unknown"`
- [ ] Jabba's mass of `"1,358"`
- [ ] R2-D2's hair color `"n/a"` (doesn't apply) versus Mace Windu's `"none"` (a real answer)
- [ ] R2-D2's gender `"n/a"` versus IG-88's `"none"`
- [ ] Multi-valued colors like `"auburn, grey"`
